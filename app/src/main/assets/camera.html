⚠ Camera access over HTTP
Most browsers require HTTPS for camera access on non-localhost origins. To enable it on your local network:
• Chrome / Edge: go to chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure, add this page's origin (e.g. ), enable & relaunch.
• Firefox: open about:config → set media.devices.insecure.enabled to true.
